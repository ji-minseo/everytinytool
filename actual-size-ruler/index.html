Helpful details

How to measure with an online ruler

This ruler is useful for a quick size estimate when you don't have a physical ruler nearby. The automatic estimate works as a starting point; card calibration improves accuracy.

1. Auto-size, then optionally calibrate

The initial estimate uses device information but does not identify the screen diagonal. Choose your display size (for example 13.3 inches) to calculate a better starting scale. For precision, match the long (85.60 mm) or short (53.98 mm) card edge and save. Use the short edge on narrow phones.

2. Pick units and direction

Select centimeters, millimeters or inches. Horizontal and vertical rulers use the same calibrated scale. The 30 cm ruler can scroll beyond the visible screen area.

3. Measure from zero

Place the start of the object at the zero mark. Tap or click the ruler at the other end to see its length in mm, cm and inches. Arrow keys adjust the selected marker when the ruler is focused.

4. Understand accuracy limits

Actual size is not guaranteed without calibration. Even after calibration, screen zoom, viewing angle, card alignment and different monitor density can introduce errors. Use a physical measuring tool for precision or safety-critical work.

Online ruler FAQ

Is the screen ruler accurate on every phone?

No. Screen pixel density and browser zoom vary between devices. The ruler starts with a device-family estimate. Browsers cannot reliably tell whether a laptop screen is 13, 13.3, or 13.6 inches. Selecting the known screen diagonal gives a more useful starting point, but does not guarantee accuracy. Calibrate with a known physical reference to make screen markings more useful, and verify accuracy with a real ruler if precision matters.

Can I measure in both centimeters and inches?

Yes. Choose cm, mm or inch at the top. The ruler redraws its tick marks, and a selected measurement is shown in all three units.

Why use a credit or debit card for calibration?

Most standard bank cards use the ID-1 size, 85.60 × 53.98 mm, so they provide a convenient reference. Specialty cards and protective cases may have different dimensions, so check the card you use.

Does this work without installing an app?

Yes. Use it in a modern desktop or mobile browser. Your display size is not sent to a server. Calibration is saved locally in your browser where supported.

What happens if I change browser zoom or monitors?

Zoom and device changes can invalidate your saved scale. When the browser reports a screen-size or pixel-ratio change, the tool falls back to a fresh automatic estimate. If you previously selected a screen diagonal, select it again or recalibrate. Some display changes cannot be detected, so check with a card when you move to another monitor.

Why is a 30 cm ruler longer than my phone screen?

A 30 cm physical ruler is longer than most phone screens. Swipe or scroll along the ruler to reach the remaining tick marks, or rotate to a vertical ruler. Scrolling doesn't change the scale.
