Compress video or change its format in a browser

Select a short clip, choose the target width and bitrate, and export in one of the browser's supported encodings. Smaller resolutions and lower bitrate settings usually reduce the file size, but they can reduce visual quality, and the final size cannot be guaranteed.

How does conversion work?

Video frames are decoded and re-encoded through the browser's video and MediaRecorder APIs. This is genuine re-encoding rather than changing a filename extension. Which input files and output formats work depends on your browser and operating system. The process happens in real time; keep this tab active. This tool is designed for short videos up to five minutes, not large film files.

Video processing uses the user's browser. If the recorder cannot capture audio, the output may be silent; check the downloaded result. For reliable batch conversion across every codec and long clips, use a dedicated desktop video editor.

Video Studio FAQ

Can I convert MP4 to WebM?

Yes, if your browser can decode your MP4 and offers WebM recording. The tool re-encodes the frames as WebM.

Can I export MP4?

Only if your browser supports MP4 encoding through MediaRecorder. Unsupported formats are not offered; changing a file extension alone is not a video conversion.

How much can I compress a video?

That depends on the content, duration, dimensions and bitrate. A smaller bitrate may help reduce size at the cost of quality.

Do video files leave my device?

No. The selected video is processed locally through browser APIs. The site does not upload video data to its servers.

Why does compression take so long?

Browser-native recording replays the full video stream. A two-minute video can take roughly two minutes to encode.
