Clean up a messy list.

Paste names, keywords or notes. Trim extra spaces, remove repeated or blank lines, and sort if you want. Keep the result ready to copy without signing up.

Runs in your browser · No text upload needed

Duplicates are compared without case or outer spaces. The first spelling is kept.

No lines yet

Paste your text or try an example to begin.

Useful for

Preparing a guest list, collecting survey answers, tidying product names or removing duplicate keywords before putting a list into another app.

What stays unchanged?

Each remaining line keeps its original wording unless you choose trimming. Sorting is optional, so you can preserve the original order. Nothing is saved to an account.
