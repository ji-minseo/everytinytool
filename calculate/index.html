Every Tiny Tool · Calculate FAQ

Is Every Tiny Tool · Calculate free?

Yes. All nine current calculators are free to use without creating an account.

Do I need to install an app?

No. Open a calculator in your browser on desktop or mobile, enter your values and select Calculate.

Which calculators are available?

You can calculate percentages, age, discounts, dates, averages and statistics, tips and bill splits, time, ratios and VAT.

Can I check how a result was calculated?

Yes. Each calculator displays its calculation formula below the result. Date and age calculations also explain the counting conventions used.

Does the business-day calculator automatically exclude public holidays?

No. It can exclude Saturdays and Sundays, and you can enter custom holiday dates. Public holidays are not loaded automatically; holidays that overlap with excluded weekends are not subtracted twice.

Can I use it for work, shopping or studying?

Yes. Calculate work hours after breaks, compare sequential discounts, split a bill, find a date interval or check statistics. For official or high-stakes decisions, verify the inputs, rounding and applicable rules independently.
