Random Group Generator

Use this group shuffler to paste a list, choose how many people or items should go in each group, and split everything into random groups instantly.

The final group may be smaller when the list does not divide evenly.

Balanced group shuffler

Choose the maximum size you want. The tool creates enough groups and distributes items as evenly as possible so you do not end up with one tiny leftover group.

Different from team count

If you know the number of teams instead, use the Team Generator. Here you control the size of each group rather than the total number of groups.

Good for classes and activities

Use it for breakout sessions, table assignments, workshops, study groups, games or any activity that needs quick random grouping.
