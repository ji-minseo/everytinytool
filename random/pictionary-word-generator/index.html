360 built-in prompts

Choose words or phrases across six categories and three difficulty levels, with enough variety for longer game sessions.

No repeats until the pool is used

Prompts are shuffled into a deck for your current filters. A prompt will not repeat until that filtered deck has been exhausted.

Timer included

Run 30, 60, 90 or 120 second rounds directly on the page, with a short sound when time is up.

How to run a five-round game

Select Easy for younger or new players, choose All categories and set the timer to 60 seconds. Reveal one prompt for each round. Award one point when the group guesses before the countdown ends, then hand the drawing role to the next player.

Adjust the difficulty for your group

Easy is a sensible warm-up; Medium and Hard add challenge. The Animals, Food, Places, Actions, Objects and People filters let a classroom or party choose a narrower theme. You can generate 1, 3, 5 or 10 prompts per reveal.

Can a prompt repeat?

Prompts are drawn without repeats within the selected filtered deck until it has been exhausted. Changing filters changes the eligible pool. To start a fresh game, use Reset used prompts. For nouns, verbs and adjectives outside a drawing game, try the Random Word Generator.
