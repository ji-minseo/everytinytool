Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026.

Every Tiny Tool is designed to keep simple utilities simple. This policy explains what information may be processed when you use everytinytool.com and its related tool collections.

Information you provide to tools

Many tools process values, text or image files directly in your browser. When a tool says that it runs locally or in your browser, the input for that tool is not intentionally uploaded to an Every Tiny Tool server for processing.

Please avoid entering sensitive personal information into any public web utility unless it is necessary for the task.

Technical and usage information

Like most websites, hosting providers, analytics services or security services may process basic technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, referring page, pages visited and approximate timestamps. This information may be used to operate the site, understand traffic and improve reliability.

Cookies and local storage

Every Tiny Tool may use cookies or browser storage for site functionality, analytics, preferences or advertising. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling some storage may affect certain features.

Advertising

Every Tiny Tool may display advertising, including advertising provided by Google AdSense or other advertising partners. Advertising providers may use cookies, web beacons or similar technologies to deliver, measure and improve ads, subject to their own privacy policies and applicable consent requirements.

Google explains how it uses information from sites and apps that use its services in its own privacy and advertising documentation.

Consent in regulated regions

Where required by law, advertising or analytics technologies that require consent will be used only after the appropriate consent choice has been presented. The exact options shown may depend on your location and the services enabled on the site.

Third-party links

Every Tiny Tool links to related subdomains and may link to third-party websites. Those sites may have their own privacy practices, which are not controlled by this policy.

Children's privacy

Every Tiny Tool is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe personal information has been provided inappropriately, please use the contact page to report it.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the site, analytics or advertising setup changes. The latest version will remain available on this page with an updated date.

Contact

For privacy questions or site feedback, please use the Contact page.
