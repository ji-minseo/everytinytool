How to calculate a tip

Multiply your bill by the tip percentage divided by 100. For example, a 20% tip on a $50 bill is $50 × 0.20 = $10. Add the tip for a $60 total. If two people are sharing equally, each person pays $30.

In the Tip tab above, enter the bill, tip percentage and number of people. Select Calculate to see the total tip, amount owed by each person and the formula. In the Split bill tab, you can divide any total and optionally round up each person's share.

How much is a 15% tip?

For an $80 bill, multiply 80 by 0.15. The tip is $12 and the total with tip is $92. Change the percentage to calculate any rate.

Calculate a 20% tip

A $50 bill with a 20% tip adds $10, making the total $60. Split equally among three people, that's $20 per person.

Split and round up a bill

Splitting 73,510 among three people gives about 24,503.33 each. Rounding each share up to the next 100 makes it 24,600 per person, with 290 collected above the original bill.

Tip Calculator FAQ

How do I calculate a tip?

Multiply the bill amount by the tip percentage, then divide by 100. For a $50 bill at 20%, the tip is $10. Add the tip to the bill to get $60.

How much is a 15%, 18% or 20% tip on $50?

On $50, a 15% tip is $7.50, an 18% tip is $9, and a 20% tip is $10. The totals including tip are $57.50, $59 and $60, respectively.

How do I split a bill including tip?

First calculate the tip, add it to the bill, then divide the total by the number of people. For a $50 bill with a 20% tip shared by two people, each person pays $30. The Tip tab calculates this directly.

Should I enter the bill before or after tax?

Enter whichever bill amount you want to base the tip on. The calculator uses exactly the number entered and does not add taxes automatically; local tipping customs can vary.

Can I round up each person's payment?

Yes. Open Split bill and choose a rounding unit such as 10 or 100. The tool shows the per-person amount and any extra collected by rounding up.

Is this tip calculator free?

Yes. Tip and bill-splitting calculations are free, with no account or app installation required.
