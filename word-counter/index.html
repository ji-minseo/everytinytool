The details

Understand the length of your draft

Checking length while writing is useful for a short description, a school assignment, an email or a presentation script. These counters measure slightly different things.

Words vs. characters

A word count estimates separate written words, while character count includes spaces and punctuation. For example, “Hello world!” has 2 words and 12 characters, including the space and exclamation mark.

Characters without spaces

The space-free count removes whitespace, including spaces, tabs and line breaks. In “Hello world!” that leaves 11 characters. It still includes punctuation marks.

Lines and paragraphs

Lines are separated by line breaks, including empty lines. Paragraphs are blocks of text separated by one or more blank lines. A line break on its own does not start a new paragraph.

Reading-time estimate

The time estimate assumes 200 words read per minute. Actual reading speed depends on your audience, language, subject and formatting. The estimate is a planning aid, not a prediction.

Common questions

Does punctuation count as a character?

Yes. Punctuation marks count, as do spaces in the full character total. The without-spaces total excludes whitespace but keeps punctuation.

Does the counter support multiple languages?

Yes, it works with Unicode text and uses the browser’s word and character segmentation when supported. Word boundaries can differ from word processors, particularly for languages that do not usually use spaces.

Is my writing saved or uploaded?

No. Text is analyzed in the browser and is not uploaded to an Every Tiny Tool server by this tool. Avoid sharing sensitive material on public websites.
