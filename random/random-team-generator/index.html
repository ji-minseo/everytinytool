Random Team Generator

Balanced random teams

Paste the names, choose how many teams you need and shuffle. People are distributed round-robin so team sizes stay as even as possible.

Team count or team size

Split by the exact number of teams or by how many people should be on each team. Team sizes stay within one person when possible.

Adjust teams by dragging

After shuffling, drag a name from one team to another for final manual changes, then copy the adjusted result.
